# ML pipeline

No dataset or trained model is bundled. Do not add synthetic student records or publish performance claims. Place a permissioned, documented CSV in `ml/data/student_wellness.csv`; keep it out of version control. The expected features are numeric scales `mood`, `energy`, `sleep_quality`, `workload`, `concentration`, `stress`, `social_connection`, `study_hours`, and `break_frequency` (each scale must be defined in the dataset card). `target` is a dataset-defined label; document its meaning, provenance, consent, and license before training.

### Open research dataset reviewed

The [SSAQS student stress and anxiety dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/18706837) is available under CC BY 4.0, and its accompanying [Scientific Data paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07085-7) describes consent, ethics approval, anonymization, daily self-reports, and wearable records. A schema inspection found 35 participant folders. Its self-report file contains timestamps plus stress and anxiety ratings; physiological/activity data are in separate Fitbit-derived files. Those inputs do not match BrainBot's current check-in features, and the cohort is too small and narrow to validate this app's general wellness estimate. It is therefore documented as a research candidate, not used to train or enable the production-facing model. Keep downloaded research data out of the public repository.

`training.train` validates labels/features, splits train/validation/test rows before fitting imputers/encoders, performs stratified cross-validation for candidate classifiers, and reports actual held-out accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC where defined, and confusion matrix. It trains Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM and probability averaging, selects a candidate on validation macro-F1, and saves the complete preprocessing/model pipeline plus metadata. A locked test set is used once for reporting. Check class imbalance and calibration before deployment; the initial pipeline is not validated for clinical use or demographic fairness.

Artifacts are versioned local files and are not committed. Inference loads only `MODEL_DIR/model.joblib`; the service does not accept a caller-supplied path. Explanations use SHAP permutation attribution against a stored training background sample. They are model feature contributions, not causal explanations, and are unavailable unless a compatible artifact and SHAP runtime are installed. Model output is not a diagnosis and should not be deployed until data governance, evaluation, calibration, bias assessment, and human review are complete.
